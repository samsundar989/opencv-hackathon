Camera health

USGS runs about 1,300 river cameras and nobody watches them. River Steward checks every served camera from its own recent frames, the same OpenCV measurements the live pipeline makes: is it offline, has the view moved, is IR stuck on, is the lens degrading, does the camera disagree with the gauge? Each finding is a ticket draft. Nothing is sent anywhere until an operator approves it.
