Live: 20 USGS river cameras read as water-level gauges

Each camera's newest frame is registered to its own reference with OpenCV 5 (ALIKED + LightGlue + MAGSAC). A per-site scene model, calibrated from the camera's own USGS gauge history, then reads the river stage from the picture. Blue in the images shows what is under water at that stage. When a gauge goes offline, the camera keeps reading (marked provisional).
