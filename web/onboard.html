Add a camera

Any USGS HIVIS camera that sits next to a stream gauge can calibrate itself from the gauge's past year: no staff plate, no survey, no per-site tuning. Pick a camera and request it. An operator approves the request on the approvals page; only then does the onboarding pipeline run, once, on the RiverSight compute box. The camera goes live only if its forward-chaining test reaches tier B.
