Public data feed

Read-only static JSON and an Atom feed, refreshed by the live worker every cycle (15 min). No key, CORS open. Values are camera-derived estimates from RiverSight's live models next to the USGS gauge; they are not official USGS data. Images are face-blurred derivatives of USGS HIVIS frames (public domain).

Endpoints

pathwhatrefresh
/api/v1/sites.jsonevery served camera: location, group (frozen20 | onboarded), status, latest valueseach cycle
/api/v1/sites/<cam_id>/latest.jsonthe latest reading for one cameraeach cycle
/api/v1/sites/<cam_id>/history.jsonthe last 7 days of readingshourly
/api/v1/feed.xmlAtom: flood-state changes (normal / elevated / flood) and operator-approved flood alertseach cycle

Fields

fieldmeaning
camera_stage_ft, camera_sigma_ftStage read from the image (scene model + Kalman), ± the site's year-long out-of-fold error. null at night, when the frame does not register, or above the calibrated range.
camera_at_least_ftSet instead of a number when the river is above anything the camera saw in calibration: the stage is at least this high.
calibrated_range_ft[min, max] stage in the camera's calibration data.
gauge_stage_ft, gauge_time, gauge_age_minThe co-located USGS gauge (NWIS IV) as seen by the cycle.
fused_stage_ft, fused_sigma_ft, fused_sourceHeadline "RiverSight stage": the gauge when fresh, a camera-driven gap-fill when not (present once enabled).
flood_statenormal | elevated (≥ calibration p90) | flood (≥ p99) | unknown.
statusok, provisional (gauge down), above_range, view_change, quarantined, night, stale, …
flagsVIEW_CHANGE, LOW_QUALITY, GAUGE_STALE, DISAGREE, RISING, OUT_OF_RANGE, NIGHT.
overlay_url, thumb_urlThe frame with the area under water at the estimated stage (connected inundation), face-blurred.
groupfrozen20: the 20 evaluated cameras. onboarded: added Oct 24–25 with the frozen setting, not part of the frozen test.

Not for life-safety decisions. Use official NWS warnings and USGS data for those.
