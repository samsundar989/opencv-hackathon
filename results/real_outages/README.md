- Gauges with 00065 data in the window: **875** (of 1077 distinct gauges behind gauged HIVIS cameras).
- Gauges with at least one outage ≥ 6 h (gap, USGS-estimated backfill, or equipment/ice-qualified run): **531** (61%).

- By kind (raw runs before merging): gap 3629, qualified 143, estimated 135.
- Camera frames checked for 1260 outages (cached listings or one NIMS listing each): **795** had ≥ 3 daylight camera frames while the gauge was out (63% of those checked).
